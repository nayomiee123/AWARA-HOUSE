Never Heard of Awara?

Awara is a Nigerian soybean-based food made much the same way tofu is. It is soaked, blended, curdled and pressed, then cut into pieces and fried until the outside turns a deep golden brown while the inside stays soft and light.

It's especially popular in Northern Nigeria, where it's sold hot off the fire as a street snack. At Awara House, we've brought that same soulful, home-style Awara down to Uyo, so you don't have to travel up North to taste it.

From Soybeans to Awara

  1. 01

    Soak

    Soybeans are soaked until they soften.

  2. 02

    Blend

    The softened soybeans are blended with water.

  3. 03

    Extract

    The mixture is strained to separate the soy milk.

  4. 04

    Curdle

    A coagulant is added and the soy milk separates into curds.

  5. 05

    Press

    The curds are pressed to remove excess water.

  6. 06

    Fry

    The Awara is cut into pieces and fried until golden.
